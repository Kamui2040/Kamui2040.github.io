I've released a pin-up magazine cover replacer for Fallout 4 with 134 new covers in total: 130 for the base game, Far Harbor and Nuka-World, plus four for optional Creation Club content. Standard and high-resolution versions are available.

Optional Creation Club packs are also available for Halloween Workshop (one cover) and Enclave Remnants (three covers), both in standard and high resolution.

Here are a few of the more restrained covers from the collection. These are artwork previews, not in-game screenshots.

The covers were made with AI-assisted artwork and adapted to fit the magazines. If you enjoy the mod, an endorsement on Nexus is always appreciated.
