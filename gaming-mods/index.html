Dead Island: Riptide

DIRDE UE Linux 0.1.0.dev0

Native Linux port of the original DIRUE mod menu.

  • Simple GUI with 42 released non-default gameplay options from the original DIRUE menu.
  • Lower sprint and jump stamina costs, run while holding weapons, improved movement, more ammo, larger inventory, and increased weapon durability.
  • Better loot, bullet penetration, instant door breaking, 90% less sunflare, removed reverb and echo, and skipped intro videos.
  • Vehicle NoClip, camera FOV options (72 and 82), better firearm upgrades, and firearm aiming/POV tuned for each FOV.
  • Zombie difficulty options (One Hit, Hard, Headshot Only) and zombie sizes from Extra Small to Supersize.
  • Forced spawns for Butchers, Rams, Bloaters, Thugs, Suiciders, armed bandits, and melee bandits.
  • Weather and time options including night, rain, storms, and darker-night variants.
  • Safe Data0 patching with validation and a pristine backup, exact restore support, and Flatpak/AppImage packages for x86-64 Linux.
  • No Wine or Proton required.

Credit to FireEyeEian for the original DIRUE project.
