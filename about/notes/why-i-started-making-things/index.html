I'm K2040, an independent developer. I make Android apps, mod games, and write fantasy. Each of those started with something I wanted to do for myself.

I started making Android apps because I kept struggling to find free, privacy-focused apps that weren't full of ads or tracking services. Eventually, I decided to try building my own instead of settling for something I didn't really want to use.

Modding games came from a similar place. Sometimes a game was missing a feature I wanted; other times I had an idea for a different way to do something. Making mods lets me fill those gaps and give familiar things my own twist.

Writing has been a much longer-held ambition. For decades, I've dreamed of writing a fantasy story of my own. Starting my light novel, Telanas - the dragon knight, was my way of finally doing something about that dream.

Those are the main things I work on today. I'll use this space for occasional thoughts, experiments, and stories behind the projects. News and release details will stay on their own pages.
