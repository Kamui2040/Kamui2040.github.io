Mark items as junk or protected, maintain blacklists and whitelists, and optionally automate selling, destruction, disenchanting and milling. The addon works on its own; AddOnSkins enables its optional ElvUI presentation, and AdiBags integration is available. Install it in Interface/AddOns and configure it from the game's Interface settings.
