# Asset provenance

The website's K2040 wordmark, two-quarter frame, mobile corner frames and favicon were created from project-owner-approved K2040 relaunch artwork. No third-party stock imagery or copyrighted game screenshots are included in these files.

The wordmark is assets/k2040-approved-horizontal-wordmark.avif, SHA-256 21c6be8722955f64919535dfce03ed50c5de2afeec0595009397782f0450d0d6, 856 x 245. The SVG frame assets/k2040-two-quarter-frame.svg preserves the approved two-quarter geometry. The files assets/k2040-mobile-corner-light.svg and assets/k2040-mobile-corner-dark.svg use the corresponding minimal corner motif. The file assets/icons/k2040-home.webp is the project's favicon.

The wordmark and frame identity may not be represented as an external brand's assets. Owner-held editable originals are not in this public repository.
